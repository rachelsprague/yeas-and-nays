Kinds of vote

Each roll call's kind comes from the question voted on, using the same definitions as its vote page.

Passage
Votes on passing a measure or agreeing to a resolution, including under suspension of the rules, agreeing to the other chamber's version (a motion to concur) and overriding a veto. Rules (resolutions setting how the House debates a measure) are counted as procedural.
Amendments
Votes on agreeing to an amendment.
Nominations
Senate votes on confirming a nomination. A cloture vote on a nomination is counted as procedural.
Procedural and other
All other questions: rules, ordering the previous question, cloture, motions to proceed, table, recommit, commit or discharge, waivers, quorum calls and the election of the Speaker, among others.
